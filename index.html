I'm Abdulla Al Saba, a MERN Stack Developer who enjoys turning ideas into clean, responsive, and scalable web applications. What pulled me into programming wasn't a single moment — it was the slow realization that every website I used was just logic and structure someone had carefully put together, and I wanted to learn how to build that myself.

I work across the full stack: on the backend, I design REST APIs, build secure JWT and OAuth authentication flows, and architect MongoDB schemas with normalized collections and relational queries. I'm currently pursuing a BSc in Chemistry at Dhaka Central University, but software development is where I direct most of my time and energy — it's a deliberate choice, not a backup plan. I care about writing code that's easy to reason about, not just code that works.
